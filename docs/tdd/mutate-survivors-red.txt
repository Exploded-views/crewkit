# tools/mutate.py (issue 88): two mutants of tests/mutants/mutate.json that survived the Linux gate, and the tests that kill them now.

Gate run, mutate.json in the Linux container under load:
  SURVIVED     flush removed
  SURVIVED     second signal not ignored during cleanup

"second signal not ignored during cleanup": an equivalent mutant for the old test since fix round 3. The KILL after the
2 s grace is in a finally, so a second signal during the grace still ends in KILL and cleanup with or without the
SIG_IGN. The new test, test_the_first_signal_makes_every_handled_signal_ignored, checks the first signal leaves every
handled signal ignored. On the mutant:
  FAIL: test_the_first_signal_makes_every_handled_signal_ignored
  FAILED (failures=1)

"flush removed": a race in test_lines_are_flushed_when_stdout_is_a_file. Its hang slept 30 s and the test waited 30 s,
so under load a late wake read the file after mutate.py had exited, and the exit flushed the line. Alone, the old test
kills it (container: Ran 1 test in 30.123s, FAILED). The hang now sleeps 300 s (mutant_timeout 120), and the test checks
mutate.py was still running when the line was read.
